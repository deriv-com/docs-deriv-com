# Spark pillars (primary pillar required)

1) How We Build
- Execution, systems, decision-making in action (war rooms, migrations, process redesign).
- Lens: constraint solved? structural change? tension exposed?

2) AI & Innovation
- Capability shifts, internal tools, platform/architecture evolution.
- Lens: capability unlocked? skill gap revealed? incremental vs strategic?

3) Leadership & Direction
- Strategic signals, structural changes, priorities.
- Lens: what does this signal? who is affected? trade-offs?

4) Product, Market & Partner
- External impact: launches, partners, compliance shifts.
- Lens: customer/partner effect? competitive problem solved? risk mitigated?

5) People & Culture
- Human side of progress; identity and culture signals.

6) Learning & Development
- Capability building; training tied to performance outcomes.

Decision guide:
- Structure/process changed → How We Build
- Capability changed → AI & Innovation
- Direction changed → Leadership & Direction
- External trust/growth changed → Product, Market & Partner
- Identity/culture changed → People & Culture
- Skill level changed → Learning & Development
