# Structured data

- `disclosures.csv`: exact source wording, routing triggers, and release notes for 14 footer profiles.
- `claims.csv`: expired, conflicting, or example-only campaign claims. No row in this release is a general reusable claim approval.
- `terminology.csv`: selected high-value editorial, product, Wallet, and partner terms.

Do not add or promote a row until its owner, status, market, effective dates, source, and platform approval are known. Treat loss percentages, company wording, links, campaign values, and eligibility as volatile.
