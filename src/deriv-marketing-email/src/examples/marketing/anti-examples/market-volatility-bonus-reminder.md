# Anti-example: market-volatility-led bonus reminder

## Source status

The source explicitly labels this first reminder `Don't use`.

## Problematic pattern

The email promotes a credit bonus by claiming that extra margin gives a strategy “breathing room”, quantifies market control through leverage, cites recent gold and oil moves, and suggests that extra capital enables wider stop-losses to survive reversals.

## Why the system must reject it

- It uses volatile market facts that expire quickly.
- It connects leverage and extra margin to improved strategy resilience.
- It may encourage greater exposure during volatile markets.
- It risks presenting market commentary as a reason to trade.
- It can become inaccurate between drafting and sending.
- It was explicitly marked not for use.

## Safer direction

Use only the approved campaign mechanics, eligibility, deadline, conditions, and action. Do not add market-event urgency or outcome implications unless a current approved source and Compliance explicitly authorise them.
