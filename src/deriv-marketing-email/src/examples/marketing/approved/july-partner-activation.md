# Historical pattern: partner activation campaign

## Metadata

- Source ID: SRC-007
- Audience: Business partners
- Channels: Email and WhatsApp
- Type: Client-acquisition campaign announcement
- Offer expiry: 31 July 2026
- Status: Time-limited example only

## Email pattern

**Subject:** A new promo for July for more clients

The message tells partners what the campaign is for, summarises the referred-client offer, links to more information and ready-to-use assets, and invites questions.

## WhatsApp pattern

The shorter version leads with the client offer, states the deadline, and points to downloadable campaign assets.

## Reusable lessons

- Tell the partner how the campaign supports their work.
- Separate the partner benefit from the referred-client offer.
- Provide an authoritative details destination and ready-to-use assets.
- Adapt the same hierarchy to a shorter channel.

## Current-style correction

Use descriptive links such as `Read the campaign details` and `Download the campaign assets`; do not use `Learn more here` or `Click here to download`.

## Do not reuse

The offer value, account type, eligibility, reward cap, dates, and URLs without current campaign-owner confirmation.
