# Historical pattern: first-deposit bonus sequence

## Metadata

- Source ID: SRC-005
- Audience: ROW V2 clients
- Type: Promotional activation sequence
- Status: Historical example only
- Offer expiry: 1 April 2026
- Reusable facts: None

## Sequence pattern

### Initial automation

**Subject:** Get 20% extra on your first MT5 transfer  
**Preview:** Trade larger positions on Forex and Gold with up to $100 in extra margin.  
**Heading:** 20% extra margin, automatically

The email introduces the offer, explains the qualifying first Wallet-to-MT5 transfer, provides a concrete cap, states the expiry, uses one deposit CTA, and keeps the withdrawal condition close to the offer.

### First reminder

The reminder restates the offer in less detail, broadens the product context only where supported, foregrounds the deadline, and keeps one action.

### Second reminder

The second reminder makes the deadline prominent and reduces educational detail. It repeats eligibility and activation steps before the CTA.

### Post-deposit email

The email recognises that the Wallet is already funded and changes the action from depositing to transferring to MT5. This is a useful lifecycle principle: adapt the CTA to the reader's confirmed state rather than repeating the original acquisition message.

## Reusable lessons

- Use customer-state data to change the next action.
- Keep offer value, cap, eligibility, expiry, and withdrawal condition together.
- Build a sequence with progressively less explanation and more action clarity.
- Use one primary CTA per stage.

## Do not reuse

- The 20%, $100, leverage, account eligibility, or 1 April 2026 details.
- Statements that extra margin creates resilience, better positioning, or more successful trading.
- Historical footer wording.
- The market-news-led variants excluded from the positive set.
