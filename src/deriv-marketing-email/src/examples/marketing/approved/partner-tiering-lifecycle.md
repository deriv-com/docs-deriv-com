# Historical pattern: partner tiering lifecycle

## Metadata

- Source ID: SRC-008
- Audience: Business partners
- Types: Tier announcement, statement, action required, correction, quarterly milestone
- Status: Pattern only; current programme rules require a live source

## Useful patterns

### Tier announcement

Recognise the partner's current status, state the benefits in a short list, explain the next attainable tier, and set expectation for the statement that follows.

### Monthly statement

Present the current tier, rolling three-month commission, current-month commission, reward amount, and credit date in a consistent list. Keep progression guidance separate from the factual statement.

### Action required

When a reward exists but the partner lacks the required account, lead with the earned status and explain the single setup step needed to receive current and future rewards.

### Correction

State the error plainly, instruct the reader to disregard the earlier email, present the corrected facts, and explain whether any money, status, or future communication is affected.

### Quarterly milestone

Use a personal congratulatory note, state the qualification period and credited amount, and avoid turning recognition into an unsupported promise about future earnings.
