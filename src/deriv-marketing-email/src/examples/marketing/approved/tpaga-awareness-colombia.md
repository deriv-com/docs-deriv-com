# Historical pattern: Tpaga awareness in Colombia

## Metadata

- Source ID: SRC-009
- Audience: Clients in Colombia only
- Languages requested: English and Spanish
- Type: Payment-method awareness
- CTA: Deposit now
- Status: Historical pattern; product steps require current verification

## Structure

**Subject:** Pay from your phone in seconds  
**Heading:** Deposit with Tpaga, no card needed

The email opens with the payment-method value, then presents four numbered steps: select Tpaga in the Cashier, enter the local-currency amount, follow the Tpaga instructions, and confirm that the Deriv balance updates after completion. It closes with a concise deposit CTA and the client support line.

## Reusable lessons

- State the payment method and main value immediately.
- Use numbered steps for an unfamiliar process.
- Match labels to the current interface.
- End with the action that the instructions prepare the reader to take.
- Declare market and language limitations in metadata.

## Do not reuse without confirmation

- The claim that no card or bank account is required.
- Cashier category names, field labels, processing behaviour, or screenshots.
- Colombia availability in another market.
- Historical disclaimer text.
