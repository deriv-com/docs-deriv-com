# Output contract

## Default: draft and QA

### 1. Status

`blocked`, `ready_with_warnings`, or `ready_for_human_review`.

### 2. Brief interpretation

Summarise audience, objective, primary action, email class, market, sender profile, and selected disclosure profile.

### 3. Blocking and unresolved information

List missing facts, source conflicts, assumptions, severity, and required owner action.

### 4. Message strategy

State the proposition, hierarchy, approved proof points, CTA strategy, and reason to act now.

### 5. Clean email

- Subject
- Preheader, when used
- Heading
- Body
- Primary CTA label
- CTA destination
- Support line
- Selected disclosure

Do not insert QA comments inside clean copy.

### 6. Claim ledger

Use the claim-ledger template.

### 7. QA

Use the QA-report template.

### 8. Human review

Name the teams or owners required before sending.
