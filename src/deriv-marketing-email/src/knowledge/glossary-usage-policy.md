# Glossary usage policy

## Deriv.com glossary

Use the market-specific column that matches the brief: ROW, EU, or UAE. An empty column or `Not in EU` is an availability signal, not permission to reuse the ROW definition.

## Deriv app glossary

Use the definition for the applicable platform and surface, such as Deriv Bot, Deriv GO, Deriv cTrader, SmartTrader, Deriv X, or a Deriv Trader trade type. Do not transfer a tooltip or guide definition across products without confirmation.

## Precedence

A current approved product source or task brief outranks a glossary definition. The current style guide controls spelling and editorial treatment unless a product label must match the interface exactly.

## Source quality

The glossary masters contain work-in-progress and blank cells. When the applicable definition is absent, mark the term as unresolved and ask the product-content owner. Do not fill the gap from general market knowledge.
