# Localisation and global readability

- Write for a global audience, including readers who use English as an additional language.
- Use simple syntax, concrete verbs, and short paragraphs.
- Avoid idioms, wordplay, culture-specific references, and phrasal ambiguity.
- Preserve placeholders, amounts, dates, product names, eligibility, and mandatory wording during translation.
- Do not translate an official product or company name unless an approved localisation source provides the translated form.
- Confirm the target market and language before drafting market-limited content.
- Treat translated output as requiring human localisation review.

The Tpaga source is an example of a market-limited campaign: it is intended only for Colombia and requests English and Spanish. Do not generalise its payment claims or interface steps to other markets.
