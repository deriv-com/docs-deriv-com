# Writing conventions

## Language and readability

- Use British English except inside official names.
- Use short, conversational sentences and paragraphs.
- Use active voice.
- Prefer simple verbs such as `need` and `give` to formal alternatives such as `require` and `provide` when the meaning remains accurate.
- Avoid idioms, culture-specific references, redundancy, ambiguity, and filler words such as `furthermore`, `however`, and `therefore` when a direct transition works.
- Write positively when this does not conceal a restriction.

## Headings and case

- Use sentence case throughout.
- Do not end headings, titles, or standard email headers with a full stop.
- Use exclamation marks sparingly and never more than one at a time.
- Capitalise proper nouns and current product names only.

## Punctuation

- Use the serial comma in a list of three or more items.
- Use `and`, not `&`, unless an ampersand is part of an official name.
- Put a comma before a conjunction joining two independent clauses.
- Do not insert a comma between two imperative clauses.
- Use an en dash for ranges.
- Do not use quotation marks merely for emphasis.

## Dates and times

Preferred dates:

- `22 January 2026`
- `22 Jan 2026`
- `22/01/2026` only when the context makes the day-month order unambiguous

Do not use `1st`, `22nd`, or similar ordinal suffixes in dates.

Use the 12-hour format with a space before `am` or `pm`, and include `(GMT)` for a global audience when a time zone is required: `9:30 am (GMT)`.

## Numbers, percentages, and leverage

- Use numerals except when a number starts a sentence.
- Use commas for numbers over three digits, except proper names and leverage ratios.
- Use the `%` symbol.
- Write leverage as `1:1000`, without a thousands separator.
- Put a space between a number and a unit of measure.

## Currency

Use a three-letter currency code after the amount: `1,000 USD`, `5 EUR`, `0.05 BTC`.

When spelling out a currency, use lowercase unless it is part of a proper name. Use `US dollar Wallet` or `USD Wallet`, not `US Dollar Wallet` or `usd Wallet`.

## Links

- Never use `click here` as link text.
- Link a descriptive noun or destination, such as `Read the full terms` or `Visit the Help centre`.
- Before launch, replace internal numbered link placeholders with functional jurisdiction-specific URLs.

## Lists

Introduce a list with a colon only when the lead-in is a complete sentence. Start every list item with a capital letter. Use terminal punctuation only for complete sentences.

## Email structure

For the general transactional template:

- Subject: aim for 40 characters or fewer.
- Header: sentence case, aim for 60 characters or fewer.
- Body: normally 1-3 short paragraphs.
- CTA: a supporting sentence and action button when needed.
- Closing: the approved client or partner support line.

Treat these as defaults, not permission to omit required information.
