# Email and campaign types

## Client marketing

- Product or feature awareness
- Payment-method awareness
- Promotional offer or activation
- Deposit or transfer conversion sequence
- Education or nurture
- Re-engagement

## Partner marketing

- Client acquisition campaign announcement
- Ready-to-use campaign assets
- Programme launch or tier announcement
- Reward or performance milestone

## Transactional and lifecycle

- Deposit, withdrawal, transfer, and account status
- Maintenance or service availability
- Monthly statement or reward breakdown
- Correction or erroneous-send notice
- Action required to access an earned amount

## Classification rule

The presence of account data does not automatically make an email transactional. A message promoting a product, offer, or trading action may still require marketing treatment. When uncertain, use marketing as the provisional disclosure classification.
