# Partners-related terms spelling

| ✅ Use this | ❌ Not this | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Partner's Hub | PH, Partners Hub | The official platform where Partners manage accounts, track earnings, and view reports. |
| Partner Academy, Deriv Partner Academy | Partners Academy, Deriv Partner Community | — |
| Partners dashboard | Partner's dashboard, Partner's Dashboard | The main view in Partner's Hub where Partners track their performance, see commission stats, access referral links and codes, and manage their partnership activity. |
| Deriv partnership programme | Deriv partnership program, Deriv Partnership programme | The system that allows individuals or businesses to collaborate with Deriv by promoting its products and earning commissions. |
| Partner tiering programme | Partner Tiering programme | A performance-based system that ranks Partners in Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum tiers based on their rolling 3-month average commission. |
| Master Partner programme | Master partner programme | A programme that lets Partners earn commissions by referring individuals to join the Deriv partnership programme. |
| Partner | Affiliate, introducing broker, IB | A general term for any individual or business collaborating with Deriv under its partnership programme to earn commissions. |
| Master Partner | Master partner, master partner | A Partner who refers individuals to join the Deriv partnership programme. |
| Sub-Partner | sub-partner | A Partner referred by a Master Partner. |
| Platinum+ | Platinum plus, platinum+ | A loyalty status for Partners who stay in the Platinum tier for a full calendar quarter and earn commissions in all three months. |
| Quarterly performance bonus | Quarterly Performance Bonus, QPB | A 10% bonus on total commissions earned in a calendar quarter, available only to Platinum+ Partners. |
| Account Manager, account manager | Account manager, Country manager, accountmanager | See usage rule below. |

## Account Manager usage rule

- Use **account manager** in running text, even when referring to Deriv (for example, "speak to your Deriv account manager" or "you can contact our account managers").
- Use **Account Manager** (capital A and M) only when it appears as a formal job title before a name or in signatures and headings (for example, "Contact Deriv Account Manager Manaf" or in an email sign-off).
- Always write it as two words, never hyphenated or as a single word.
