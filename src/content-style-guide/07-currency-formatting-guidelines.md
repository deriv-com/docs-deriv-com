# Currency formatting guidelines

## Fiat currencies

- **Symbol placement:** Place the currency symbol before the amount.
- **Currency name:** Write the ISO currency code (three-letter abbreviation) after the amount, separated by a space.

Examples:

✅ 1.000 IDR
✅ 0.0002718 MYR
✅ 100 USD
✅ 50 EUR
✅ Rp1.000
✅ RM0.0002718
✅ $100
✅ €50

### Symbol or code?

Both $1,000 and 1,000 USD are correct. Use this order to decide which.

1. **Default to the symbol.** It's shorter and reads less stiff, so it suits most of what we write — marketing copy, product interfaces, emails, articles, newsletters.
   ✅ $1,000 ✅ €50 ✅ RM250

2. **Switch to the three-letter code when the currency could be misread.** Many countries use the dollar. If a text refers to more than one dollar, or a reader can't safely assume which one we mean, use the code on at least the first mention and the symbol after that.
   ✅ "Minimum withdrawal is 100 USD." ✅ "Balances are held in USD. A $500 deposit converts at the rate shown." ❌ "Your $500 balance" in a text that also discusses Australian or Singapore dollars
   This applies to any symbol shared between currencies, not only the dollar.

3. **Use the code throughout in formal documents.** Annual reports, regulatory filings, terms and conditions and similar always use the code, never the symbol.
   ✅ "a fee of 25.00 USD applies" ❌ "a fee of $25.00 applies"

**Placement.** When you use the code, it goes after the amount with a space: 1,000 USD, not USD 1,000. When you use the symbol, it goes before with no space: $1,000.

**Instruments.** Assets quoted in USD by market convention — gold, silver, Brent, WTI — carry no ambiguity, so the symbol is fine throughout outside formal documents.

**Describing a currency rather than quoting an amount:** spell it out in lowercase. "You've changed your currency to the euro." "The Australian dollar was hit hard."

## Cryptocurrencies

- **Currency code:** Write the official abbreviation after the amount, separated by a space.

Examples:

✅ 0.05 BTC
✅ 1.2 ETH
✅ 0.003 USDT
