# Push notification guidelines

- **Be concise:** Keep titles between 30 and 50 characters and body text between 100 and 150 characters to ensure readability across devices.
- **Be relevant:** Tailor notifications to the user's actions or preferences to enhance relevance.
- **Be clear and direct:** Use straightforward language.
- **Be personal and engaging:** Personalise messages and use compelling language to capture attention.
- **Use sentence case:** Write all text in sentence case for clarity and a polished appearance.
- **Use British style:** Employ British English spelling and grammar rules.

## Examples

**Title:** Trade alert 🚀
**Body:** New opportunity in the Forex market! Check it out now and make your move.

**Title:** Trade on the go! 🚀
**Body:** Don't just watch the markets, trade them with Deriv GO.

**Title:** Never miss a market beat 📈
**Body:** Your 24/7 pass to trade synthetic indices & crypto on the go.

Deriv's tone is professional yet approachable, focusing on clarity and engagement. For more details, refer to CleverTap's guide and push notification best practices.
