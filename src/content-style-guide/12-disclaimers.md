## Email footers (jurisdiction links)

To help the Digital Marketing (DM) team use the right URLs for each jurisdiction (e.g. ROW, DIEL, DCL, etc.) when sending maintenance or system update emails:

✅ **Include**

- Footer links to:
  - Help centre
  - Privacy policy
  - Terms and conditions
- A short internal note: *For DM team use — replace with correct jurisdiction URLs before sending.*

🚫 **Don't include**

- Placeholder numbers (e.g. 1, 2, 3)
- Non-functional or duplicate links
- Any lines that would appear in the client-facing version

💡 **Remember**

- These links are for DM localisation only, not visible to clients.
- Always check the latest jurisdiction URLs with DM before launch.
- The goal: ensure every email uses the right regional URLs and avoids mismatched links.
