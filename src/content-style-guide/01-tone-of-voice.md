# Deriv's tone of voice

- **Clear and direct**
  - Use straightforward, jargon-free language that is relatable. Ensure communications are easy to understand and convey core messages efficiently.

- **Friendly and approachable**
  - Create a welcoming atmosphere through a warm, inviting tone that connects with the audience. Use language that fosters a sense of community and approachability.
  - Incorporate supportive phrases that express empathy and readiness to assist, reinforcing the idea of being a helpful partner.

- **Client-centred**
  - Centre all content around the needs and interests of the audience, emphasising how Deriv can enrich their experience.
  - Communicate key information clearly, ensuring audiences feel supported and informed at every stage of their interaction with Deriv.

- **Trustworthy and transparent**
  - Maintain honesty in all communications by being upfront about processes, potential risks, and any relevant guidelines. Strive for transparency to foster trust.
  - Ensure consistent messaging across all platforms, reinforcing reliability and the Deriv brand identity.

- **Informative and engaging**
  - Aim to educate and inform by sharing valuable insights that improve understanding and decision-making. Highlight relevant topics that cater to the audience's interests.
  - Encourage interaction and engagement, inviting feedback and making communication feel more like a two-way conversation.
