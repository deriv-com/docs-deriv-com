# Email — maintenance (to inform regarding downtimes)

See also: Email (ROW, EU, UAE) - Maintenance email master template *(internal doc — link from your wiki)*

**Subject line:**
[Platform] system maintenance — [date]

**Header:**
[Platform] system maintenance

**Body:**
- [Intro paragraph — what's happening, when]
- [2nd paragraph — what's affected, what's not]
- [3rd paragraph — assurance that we'll be back online when it's done]

**Closing:**
Need help? Our support team is available 24/7 via live chat and WhatsApp.

---

## Sample

**Subject line:** cTrader system maintenance — 4 Nov 2023

**cTrader system maintenance**

We will be performing maintenance on Saturday, 4 November 2023, from 7:00 am to 10:00 am GMT.

During this time:

- The Deriv cTrader dashboard and trading on Deriv cTrader will be intermittently unavailable. Check your open positions before the maintenance starts and modify them if needed. You won't be able to modify them during the maintenance period.
- Deposits and withdrawals will be unavailable for your Deriv cTrader account.

Our services will resume as soon as maintenance is complete.

Need help? Our support team is available 24/7 via live chat and WhatsApp.
