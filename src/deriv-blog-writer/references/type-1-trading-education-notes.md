## What's known about Type 1

**Purpose (inferred from notes):** Educational trading content — broader concepts, not a single product walkthrough (e.g. comparing instrument types, explaining a trading concept).

**Mandatory requirements:**
- A **"Deriv-specific angle" section is mandatory** — this is what differentiates the article from generic trading content. Do not omit or thin this out.
- **Tables work well for direct comparisons** — side-by-side dimensions such as volatility, trading hours, risk type, etc. Use a table where the article compares two or more instruments/products across multiple attributes.
- **FAQ section is required** for this type (unlike Type 2, where FAQ is optional). Include 3–5 questions.
- The article must **teach something the reader didn't already know from the headline** — not just restate what the headline promises.
