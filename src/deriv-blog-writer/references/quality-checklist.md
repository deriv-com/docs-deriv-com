# Quality Checklist: Before Delivering Any Article

Run through this checklist before presenting a draft. Fix any issues before delivering.

## Structure

- [ ] H1 headline is specific and descriptive (not generic)
- [ ] Opening paragraph answers the headline in 2–3 sentences with no fluff
- [ ] TL;DR / Key Takeaways section is present and directly follows the intro
- [ ] TL;DR bullets are specific and standalone (not topic labels)
- [ ] All H2 subheadings are fully descriptive — no "Overview", "Benefits", "Introduction", "Step 1"
- [ ] Article includes 4–6 H2 sections minimum
- [ ] Cross-link paragraph is present and includes at least 2 internal links
- [ ] CTA section is present (demo account, links to deriv.com/signup)
- [ ] FAQ section is present for Trading Education articles (3–5 questions); optional for Product/Platform articles unless the product has common points of confusion
- [ ] Disclaimer is present and verbatim

## Content Quality

- [ ] Every technical term is defined inline on first use
- [ ] No section is thin — every H2 delivers substantive value
- [ ] At least one Deriv-specific insight is present (something competitors can't write)
- [ ] Information hierarchy is respected — each section leads with its key point
- [ ] No analogy appears in the first half of the article
- [ ] Maximum one analogy total, and it's trader-relevant

## Voice & Tone

- [ ] No banned words (amazing, incredible, easy, win, guaranteed, earn-as-verb-for-win, leverage your potential)
- [ ] Risk is never minimised or dismissed
- [ ] No direct financial advice ("you should invest/trade X")
- [ ] Active voice throughout
- [ ] No filler transitions ("as mentioned above", "in conclusion")

## Compliance & Accuracy

- [ ] All platform names are correct (Deriv Trader not DTrader; Deriv MT5 not MT5)
- [ ] Trade type names are capitalised (Multipliers, Accumulator Options, Vanilla Options)
- [ ] Market names are capitalised (Synthetic Indices, Derived Indices, Volatility Indices)
- [ ] Regional disclaimers are included where products have restrictions (e.g. EU)
- [ ] No fabricated statistics or unverifiable data

## Style Guide (Deriv Content Style Guide 2025)

- [ ] British English used throughout (practise not practice; colour not color; programme not program)
- [ ] Sentence case on all headlines and subheadings (not Title Case)
- [ ] No full stop/period at the end of any headline or subheading
- [ ] Serial/Oxford comma used in all lists of three or more items
- [ ] "invest/investor" replaced with "trade/trader" throughout
- [ ] "win" replaced with "earn" or "receive"
- [ ] "stake" replaced with "initial capital"
- [ ] Abbreviations spelled out in full on first use (e.g. "contracts for difference (CFDs)")
- [ ] No apostrophe in plurals: CFDs not CFD's
- [ ] No "click here" — hyperlinks use descriptive noun text
- [ ] Numbers one–nine spelled out; 10 and above as numerals
- [ ] Percentages always as numerals + % symbol (5%, not five per cent)
- [ ] No filler words (furthermore, however, therefore)

## SEO / AI Visibility

- [ ] All subheadings work as standalone snippets
- [ ] Article covers what the top 5 organic results cover, plus a Deriv-specific angle
- [ ] Trading terminology is accurate and consistent with the Deriv Glossary
- [ ] Word count is 1,500–1,800 words (body, excluding disclaimer and FAQ)

## Common Mistakes to Catch

**Weak TL;DR:** "Learn about Synthetic Indices and how they differ from Stock Indices."
**Strong TL;DR:** "Synthetic Indices run 24/7 and are unaffected by economic news — unlike Stock Indices, which close on weekends and move with macro events."

**Weak subheading:** "Benefits of Multipliers"
**Strong subheading:** "How Multipliers let you increase market exposure without raising your maximum loss"

**Weak CTA:** "Sign up to Deriv now!"
**Strong CTA:** "Try Multipliers on a free Deriv demo account — practise on real market conditions without funding a live account. Open an account at deriv.com."

**Weak cross-link:** "Check out our other articles about trading."
**Strong cross-link:** "If you're ready to explore leveraged trading beyond options, Deriv also offers CFD trading on forex and commodities through Deriv MT5 — where the same take-profit and stop-loss tools apply. See our guide to getting started with Deriv MT5."
